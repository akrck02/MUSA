# Projects
---

This page is for whoever draws to order with MUSA: by the end you know how a commission goes from
the first message to the sales history.

## The board

**Proyectos** is a board with one column per stage:

| Column | Means |
|--------|-------|
| Por empezar | agreed, not started |
| Dibujando | being drawn |
| Para revisar | sent to the customer for changes |
| Entregado | finished and handed over |

Each card shows a band of its colour, the title, who it is for with a button for each of their
networks, the catalogue product it makes (if any), when it is due and its price. The due date is
red when late and amber when due today or tomorrow. Due dates also show on the calendar of Inicio.

## A project

Tap «Nuevo proyecto», «Añadir proyecto» under a column, or a card, to open the editor:

- **Título**, and **Para**: the customer's name. Writing a name that already exists reuses that
  customer, with their networks.
- **Contacto** (an email or a phone) and the customer's handle on each network.
- **Precio**, **Fecha de entrega** (written as 18/11/2026) and, optionally, the digital
  **Producto** of the catalogue it makes. Physical products are not offered: a project is drawn.

«Pasar a …» moves it to the next column. Moving it to **Entregado** records its sale, with the
channel Proyecto, delivered at once, so it shows in the sales history. A delivered project cannot
move back or be deleted: undo its sale from the history instead.

## Networks

Instagram, Bluesky and X come with MUSA. Each network has a link pattern, such as
`https://x.com/{user}`, and a handle becomes a link by putting it where `{user}` is (the «@» is
left out). Tapping a network's button on a card opens the profile in the browser.

Trello will come later (proposal 4): the board is ready to mirror one, but MUSA works the same
without it.
