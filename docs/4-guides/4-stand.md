# The stand
---

This page is for whoever sells with MUSA: by the end you know how to charge a customer at a fair or at home.

## Choose where you sell

**Puesto** first asks where you are selling from:

- An **open fair**: the stand shows what you took to it, and the sales belong to the fair.
- **Todo el stock**: the stand shows every active physical product with stock, and each sale is a
  local sale (Venta local), delivered at once, so it goes straight to the sales history.

«Cambiar» goes back to this choice.

## Fill the basket

Each product is a tile: a coloured picture with its first letter (until products have photos),
its name, price and how many are left, amber when three or fewer, «Agotado» when none.

Tap a tile to add one to the basket; tap again for another. A chosen tile turns slate below, says
«en la cesta» with «−» to take one back, and its picture carries how many. The basket never holds
more than is left. The tabs on top show the products of one tag.

The bar at the bottom stays in place while the products scroll: the pieces and the total,
«Vaciar» and «Cobrar».

## Charge

«Cobrar» asks how the customer pays:

| Way | What happens |
|-----|--------------|
| Pagar en efectivo | Tap each note and coin you are handed. MUSA shows what is missing, or the change. «Confirmar» appears once there is enough. «Deshacer» takes back a mistaken tap. |
| Pagar con tarjeta | Charge on your card reader, then tap «Ha pasado». |

The sale is recorded only when you confirm. It says how it was paid, and the stock leaves the
stand or the catalogue at that moment. The last dialog shows the change to hand back.

Card payments through SumUp's own reader will come later (proposal 4).
